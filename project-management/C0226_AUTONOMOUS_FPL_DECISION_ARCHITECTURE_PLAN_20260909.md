# C0226 — Autonomous FPL Decision Architecture Program

Date: 2026-09-09 (Dubai)
Program status at creation: Planned
Objective: remove the need for a manual human red-team after the engine emits a **final** FPL decision.

## 1. Problem statement

The engine is increasingly strong at player/fixture forecasting but the current decision stack can still over-interpret tiny point-estimate differences. Recent evidence exposed the gap:

- original no-Haaland Structure D scored 240.952 over GW4–GW8;
- a structurally preferable alternative with Rogers + João Pedro and without Gabriel + Szoboszlai + Thiago scored 240.841;
- the 0.111 difference is model noise, yet a point-estimate search can present the higher decimal as superior;
- João Pedro 19.219 vs Thiago 19.118 is another statistically meaningless decimal comparison with materially different evidence/uncertainty stories;
- Gabriel at £8.0m vs Calafiori at £5.7m illustrates marginal value and club-slot opportunity cost that a static objective can underweight;
- Schade at ~4% ownership, strong minutes and meaningful haul probability should be surfaced as leverage, but not selected merely because he is unpopular.

The production target is therefore not `forecast → maximize one scalar → final squad`.

## 2. Target architecture

```text
FOOTBALL / FIXTURE / ROLE / PLAYER MODELS
        ↓
POINT + HAUL DISTRIBUTIONS
        ↓
C0227 UNCERTAINTY / SENSITIVITY STATE
        ↓
C0228 DIVERSE SEARCH + NEAR-OPTIMAL EQUIVALENCE CLASSES
        ↓
C0229 STRUCTURAL / PORTFOLIO ROBUSTNESS
        ↓
C0230 TEAM-REGIME DIAGNOSTIC (shadow until validated)
        ↓
C0231 FORWARD-MANAGEMENT / PREMIUM-ACCESS SIMULATION
        ↓
C0225 + C0232 RANK-AWARE OR / LEVERAGE CONTROL
        ↓
C0233 ADVERSARIAL RED-TEAM CHALLENGER
        ↓
C0234 FAIL-CLOSED AUTONOMOUS FINAL-DECISION GATE
        ↓
SAVED MANAGER PLAN only when separately authorized/readiness-green
```

Forecasting and decision utility remain separate. No downstream layer may rewrite xPts merely to make a preferred squad win.

## 3. Global invariants

1. Historical forecasts remain append-only.
2. ROLL/no transfer is always a comparator.
3. Current FPL rules, manager state and prices are fail-closed prerequisites.
4. Missing data is not zero.
5. No ownership/EO points multiplier.
6. No ad-hoc attacking-role points multiplier.
7. A shadow/research model cannot acquire numeric production effect through this program.
8. Statistically indistinguishable structures are an equivalence class, not a ranked list of false precision.
9. Every final recommendation must survive reasonable sensitivity perturbations.
10. A final decision can return `DECISION_NOT_READY`; autonomy means autonomous refusal when evidence is insufficient, not forced action.

## 4. C0225 — Rank-aware Differential Opportunity / Leverage

Implemented first by explicit user instruction.

Purpose: identify low-owned players with genuine football support and use leverage only to adjudicate near-equal structures.

V01 keeps the model-error band fixed. Early season remains EV-first. Later rank regimes may change tiebreak priority but do not create xPts.

Acceptance example: Schade can be tagged explosive because minutes/role/haul probability support him; a 2%-owned weak-minutes player cannot win merely from ownership.

## 5. C0227 — Model uncertainty and sensitivity state

### Purpose

Represent uncertainty about the **forecast itself**, distinct from ordinary match-outcome variance.

### Inputs

- expected minutes and P(start);
- fixture-specific role and role confidence;
- current-state sample size / stability;
- projection disagreement where available;
- fixture/model lineage completeness;
- team/fixture state freshness.

### V01 output

Do not claim a statistically calibrated confidence interval before calibration exists. V01 produces:

- explicit uncertainty-vector fields;
- LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH model-uncertainty class;
- deterministic sensitivity scenarios around minutes and attacking-event expectation;
- a squad stability report showing whether decisions survive those scenarios.

### Gate

Uncertainty may invalidate a claimed edge; it cannot manufacture an edge.

## 6. C0228 — Diverse search and equivalence classes

### Purpose

Stop treating one local-search optimum as globally authoritative.

### Search families

Generate materially different legal squads through independent deterministic seeds/anchors, including:

- balanced/value;
- premium-forward/captaincy;
- premium-midfield;
- dual-premium-midfield;
- low-cost flexibility;
- high-minutes durability.

Anchors are generated by rules/price bands and projections, never hard-coded player names.

### Output

- deduplicated candidate squads;
- structural family signature;
- objective;
- core-player overlap;
- equivalence class using the current model-error contract;
- convergence diagnostics.

A decimal difference inside the model-error band is explicitly `NO_MEANINGFUL_EDGE`.

## 7. C0229 — Structural robustness / portfolio control

### Purpose

Choose among equivalent forecasts using football and squad architecture rather than decimals.

### Controls

- role-risk and role unknowns;
- minimum xMins / weak slots;
- marginal £ value;
- club-slot opportunity cost;
- premium/captaincy access;
- useful price points and ITB;
- bench leakage;
- concentration/correlation;
- exposure to top projected attacks/defences by **asset role**, not club name alone;
- price invested in defensive vs attacking routes where the projected edge is negligible.

No control becomes an xPts coefficient. It is used only inside the equivalence/sensitivity decision process.

## 8. C0230 — Team Regime Change diagnostic

### Purpose

Detect when conservative priors may be slow to recognize a genuinely changed team, such as a new tactical/personnel regime.

### Inputs

- current-season xG/xGA/process change;
- tactical formation/role changes;
- personnel/coaching changes;
- recent team chance creation/prevention;
- fixture-adjusted evidence where available.

### Lifecycle

Shadow/diagnostic first. It may raise a red-team warning that a squad is under/overexposed to a potential regime shift. It cannot alter team lambda until a chronology-safe promotion gate is passed.

## 9. C0231 — Forward management / transfer burden

### Purpose

Evaluate ownership of a squad as a path, not a static five-GW endpoint.

### V01 simulation

For top future premium/captaincy targets, calculate:

- one-transfer reachability;
- two-transfer requirement from budget/position/club-slot constraints;
- blocked club slots;
- required downgrades;
- ITB sensitivity;
- number of fragile/likely future-transfer slots.

Wildcard evaluation must expose future transfer burden and premium-route flexibility, not only immediate fresh-squad gain.

## 10. C0232 — Rank-aware OR utility expansion

C0225 is the first production component.

C0232 later adds, when available and validated:

- effective ownership rather than raw ownership proxy;
- distance to rank target / #1;
- remaining Gameweeks;
- portfolio haul/blank covariance;
- late-season variance requirements.

It remains Decision-Control: expected points are unchanged.

## 11. C0233 — Automatic adversarial red-team

### Purpose

Automate the challenge the user currently performs manually.

For the selected structure, the challenger must attempt to produce a better alternative or a reason not to trust the choice. Mandatory questions include:

- Is an expensive defender buying enough marginal value?
- Is a selected attacker dependent on regression/uncertain finishing/penalty assumptions when an equivalent alternative has stronger evidence?
- Is the squad underexposed to a top projected attack while spending club slots elsewhere?
- Is a premium being rejected only because points-per-million favors cheap assets?
- Does an alternative inside model error improve minutes, role, leverage, flexibility or club-slot access?
- Does the selection survive xMins/role/team-strength perturbations?

Red-team output: PASS, CHALLENGED_BY_EQUIVALENT, or EDGE_NOT_ROBUST.

## 12. C0234 — Autonomous final-decision gate

A result can be labelled `FINAL_AUTONOMOUS_DECISION` only when all mandatory gates pass:

1. C0213 production/manager readiness green;
2. complete target-GW projections and roles;
3. search ensemble generated and convergence acceptable;
4. uncertainty/sensitivity does not invalidate the selected family;
5. equivalence-class adjudication complete;
6. structural robustness has no unresolved critical flag;
7. rank/leverage control applied under the correct regime;
8. forward-management burden acceptable or explicitly priced;
9. red-team PASS;
10. ROLL comparison remains favorable after transfer costs/opportunity cost;
11. chip opportunity cost separately green when a chip is proposed.

Otherwise return `DECISION_NOT_READY` or `NO_MEANINGFUL_EDGE` with the blocking reasons.

The V01 autonomous gate remains read-only. It does not write a manager plan or execute FPL changes until separately authorized and production-validated.

## 13. Permanent acceptance tests from the GW4 failure cases

### A. False precision

Original Structure D 240.952 vs revised Structure D 240.841 must be classified equivalent under a 1-point model-error band; decimal ordering alone is prohibited.

### B. Evidence-quality tie

João Pedro 19.219 vs Thiago 19.118 must not be resolved from the 0.101 mean difference. The decision stack must surface uncertainty/evidence differences and allow structural adjudication.

### C. Marginal defender value

Gabriel £8.0m vs Calafiori £5.7m must expose the £2.3m opportunity cost, club-slot consequences and alternative use of funds before an £8m defender is preferred.

### D. Differential quality

Schade (~4% ownership, strong xMins and P10+) must be surfaced as legitimate leverage. A low-owned player failing minutes/role/haul gates must not be promoted.

### E. Explosive exception

Cherki must remain discoverable as an explosive exception while his low expected minutes remain an explicit robustness risk.

### F. Wildcard humility

A raw fresh-squad search cannot be labelled final while search convergence, role risk, uncertainty, future transfer burden or red-team checks are unresolved.
