## 1. Executive decision

C0284 is a targeted end-to-end recovery of the existing engine. It is not a wholesale rebuild and does not discard the previous five weeks of work.

The immediate production failure is that the intended current-season-first generator exists and is deployed, but the active GW5-GW8 prediction snapshots are still produced by `production_fixture_v0.3_c0166`. The website therefore presents older C0166 forecasts while implying the latest engine is active.

No next-GW forecast may be published until the following are simultaneously true:

1. raw fixture totals reconcile at the forecast cutoff;
2. the validated generator is the generator that produced the active snapshot;
3. current-season evidence is the performance baseline;
4. previous-season performance follows the canonical decay and reaches 0% at nine matches;
5. no cross-season L5/L10/L20 input bypasses that contract;
6. scoring-family, result and player consumers use the same snapshot;
7. the matchup modal explains the calculations actually used;
8. the forecast is frozen and reproducible before kickoff;
9. deployed-browser QA proves the live website matches the canonical snapshot.

## 2. Confirmed findings

### 2.1 Activation and lineage

- Active GW5, GW6, GW7 and GW8 snapshots are generated by `production_fixture_v0.3_c0166`.
- `forward_fixture_v0.2.0_current_season` / C0283 is deployed but has not produced the active production cohort.
- A deployment, migration or successful CI run is not evidence of production activation.
- Forecast records used by the website remain marked `frozen=false` despite being described publicly as frozen.

### 2.2 Current-season calculation

- C0166 still consumes cross-season L5/L10 evidence through inherited C0159/C0147 paths.
- Current-season process and result adjustments are frequently extremely small or zero relative to the structural baseline.
- Current-season fact snapshots can have four result samples while a prediction manifest uses a thinner xG sample; this discrepancy is not surfaced.
- Missing or thin data can fall back silently to structural priors instead of widening uncertainty or failing closed.
- Strong attacks and mismatches remain vulnerable to lambda compression toward average outcomes.

### 2.3 High-value facts available but not used or explained well

The fixture-level data can derive decision-useful states that are absent or weakened in the modal:

- unbeaten, winless, winning and losing streaks;
- scored in every match and failed-to-score streaks;
- consecutive clean sheets and consecutive concessions;
- raw current-season totals alongside per-match rates;
- current-season xG/xGA and process-versus-finishing divergence;
- league rank for goals, xG, defence and xGA;
- home/away current-season splits when sample-qualified;
- opponent-adjusted strength and sample reliability.

Using only win rate loses important information. `1W-3D-0L` and `1W-0D-3L` must not be communicated as equivalent `won 1/4` states.

### 2.4 Result and scoring-family decisions

- The consumer converts `NO_MEANINGFUL_EDGE` into `DRAW`, even when draw is not the leading 1X2 outcome.
- Low/Normal/High family selection is implemented in the frontend rather than delivered as a canonical model decision.
- Boundary behavior can change a family label because total lambda moves by a few thousandths.
- Only the winning family is displayed; near-ties and the full family distribution are hidden.
- `4+ probability` is incorrectly used in prose as if it were the complete High-scoring-family definition.
- Shootout and demolition must remain High-scoring subtypes, not competing top-level categories.
- Outcome-coherent representative scores can differ from the largest Poisson cell without an explanation in the UI.

### 2.5 Matchup modal evidence

- A single `SUPPORTS/CONTRADICTS` axis mixes result direction, each team’s scoring opportunity, scoring environment, tactics and uncertainty.
- All ten audited modals used a generic `high scoring is not certain` risk, including Low and Normal fixtures.
- Derived model outputs are presented as independent supporting evidence.
- Research-only tactical observations are not visibly separated from production-effect inputs.
- Several modals omit important current-season facts even though the fact store contains them.
- Evidence is selected by comparative thresholds rather than decision usefulness or extremity.
- Generic `home side` / `away side` language, unexplained structural baselines and absent weights make the decision difficult to audit.
- The modal does not display capture time, snapshot ID, model version, sample mismatch or signed contribution.
- Finished-match comparison omits scoring-family, totals, BTTS and team-scoring assessment.

### 2.6 FPL propagation risk

- Match scoring environments are intended to drive player discovery, haul probabilities, captaincy nomination and chip analysis.
- Until canonical fixture activation and lineage parity are proven, player xPts and captaincy can consume stale or differently versioned fixture assumptions.
- The Top-10 xPts board is restored, but its fixture lineage must be proven rather than assumed.

## 4. Phase plan

### P0 — Active runtime, generator and trigger reconciliation

**Objective:** prove which job produces every active forecast and prevent deployed-but-inactive logic.

Actions:

- Map cron/controller/edge-function triggers that write fixture predictions.
- Identify every writer still capable of producing C0147/C0159/C0166 forecasts.
- Run the C0283 current-season-first generator in isolated shadow mode for the next-GW cohort.
- Compare active and shadow snapshot IDs, generators, inputs and timestamps.
- Add an activation-parity assertion: deployed version, invoked version, stored generator and website snapshot must agree.
- Disable or quarantine superseded writers only after the replacement passes P2.

**Gate P0:** one enumerated writer set; shadow cohort complete; no unidentified producer; activation mismatch fails closed.

### P1 — Cutoff-safe data and feature reconciliation

**Objective:** prove the inputs before assessing model quality.

Actions:

- Reconstruct matches played, goals for/against, W-D-L and xG/xGA from fixture rows at the forecast cutoff.
- Assert league-wide goals-for equals goals-against and every team total matches its fixtures.
- Reconcile result sample, xG sample and venue sample separately; never imply they are equal.
- Derive canonical current-season states: unbeaten/winless/winning/losing, scoring/blanking, clean-sheet/conceding streaks, raw totals, rates and ranks.
- Validate promoted-team coverage and block silent one-sample fallback when four result fixtures exist.
- Add an assertion protocol for disputed facts: reconstruct from underlying fixtures, distinguish pre-match from post-match, and record the cutoff before changing code or architecture.

**Gate P1:** 20/20 teams reconcile; no post-kickoff leakage; sample gaps are explicit; missing is never zero.

### P2 — Chronological engine comparison and promotion

**Objective:** select the better engine with evidence rather than activating C0283 merely because it is newer.

Actions:

- Replay GW2-GW5 using only frozen pre-kickoff information.
- Compare C0166 against the current-season-first candidate and a simple current-season baseline.
- Evaluate 1X2 Brier/log loss, team-goal MAE, total-goal calibration, family calibration, BTTS, 2+ team scoring and lambda dispersion.
- Audit prior-season contribution per team and prove the canonical schedule: maximum 25% at matches 1-4, then 20/15/10/5/0% at matches 5/6/7/8/9+.
- Reject any candidate that improves selected results but regresses calibration or relies on retrospective data.
- Promote exactly one fixture engine or fail closed; record why.

**Gate P2:** chronological benchmark passed; one registered production generator; cross-season L5/L10/L20 contribution exactly zero outside the canonical prior blend.

### P3 — Canonical decision outputs

**Objective:** move result/family meaning out of the frontend and into the versioned forecast contract.

Actions:

- Return the complete Low/Normal/High distribution from the prediction API.
- Add confidence and `NO_MEANINGFUL_EDGE` without converting it to DRAW.
- Add blended family communication for near-ties while preserving the full numeric distribution.
- Keep Shootout/Demolition as High-scoring subtypes.
- Store leading score family, raw modal cell and outcome-coherent representative score separately.
- Remove frontend family calculation and arbitrary boundary overrides.

**Gate P3:** family probabilities reconcile to the score matrix; API, FPL and UI consume the same fields; no consumer invents a result or family.

### P4 — Freeze, lineage and publication authorization

**Objective:** make the word `frozen` verifiable.

Actions:

- Create the immutable pre-kickoff snapshot before publication.
- Require `frozen=true`, cutoff, captured-at, generator, model version, feature version and decision-cycle ID.
- Block mixed-lineage fixture/player/FPL publication.
- Preserve all older snapshots append-only.
- Add a fail-closed reason visible to operators when a gate fails.

**Gate P4:** reproducible hash and snapshot lineage; no mutable or unfrozen forecast can be labelled frozen or reach the public surface.

### P5 — Decision-evidence reconstruction

**Objective:** present facts that actually help a viewer assess the forecast.

Replace the single alignment field with decision targets:

- `RESULT_DIRECTION`
- `HOME_SCORING`
- `AWAY_SCORING`
- `SCORING_ENVIRONMENT`
- `CONFIDENCE_UNCERTAINTY`

Evidence rules:

- Production reasons show only chronology-safe, material inputs with non-zero signed contributions.
- Current-season facts show raw totals plus rates where useful: e.g. `13 goals in four matches (3.25 per match)`.
- Extreme states are eligible independently of opponent comparison thresholds.
- Win/draw/loss states are communicated explicitly: `unbeaten 1W-3D-0L`, not merely `won 1/4`.
- xG/xGA and result-process divergence are included when material.
- Research-only tactics receive a separate zero-effect section.
- Model outputs are labelled projections, not independent evidence.
- Risks must target the selected decision and cannot be generic complements of support.
- Each fact exposes sample, cutoff, source, weight and signed contribution.
- A synthesis sentence explains the principal conflict and net conclusion.

**Gate P5:** contradiction tests pass; all ten next-GW modals answer what, why, risk, reliability, conflict resolution and player implication.

## 6. Autonomous execution policy

C0284 is authorized for autonomous diagnosis, implementation, migrations, tests, deployment and QA inside the existing repository and Supabase project. Routine defects are auto-fixed without user intervention.

Interrupt the user only for unavailable credentials/permissions, an irreversible external action, an unresolved requirement conflict that materially changes football decisions, or an authoritative data gap that cannot be resolved from underlying records.

Stop conditions are gate failures, not ordinary implementation problems. A gate failure blocks publication while repair continues; it does not justify silently weakening the gate.

## 7. Definition of done

C0284 is complete only when a randomly selected public number can be reconstructed:

```text
fixture rows at cutoff
→ canonical current-season/prior blend
→ signed fixture calculation
→ score matrix and scoring families
→ decision-targeted evidence
→ player/FPL propagation
→ frozen API snapshot
→ identical deployed UI
```

Passing unit tests, deploying functions, creating migrations or improving one diagnostic fixture is insufficient on its own.
